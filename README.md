One letter for a day that left your stomach unsettled: **Q, for "queasy"**. Too much bad news, too much bad coffee, a day that kept spinning until everything felt slightly off — that is a queasy day, and Q is its sharp, unmistakable initial. It lands on the judge's criteria: the word is vivid and specific (you can feel the churn, not just a vague bad mood), it is funny and surprising (nobody's first pick for a diary entry, and everyone has had that exact day), it is true to a real day rather than a performance, and Q suits "queasy" perfectly — a rare, angular letter for a queasy, off-kilter day, far from the obvious A-amazing, F-Friday or Z-zero-sleep picks.
